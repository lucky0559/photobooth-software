# Pricing and scope notes

Carried over from the "Philippine Photobooth Startup Pricing" thread (Nov 2026). Estimates only, based on typical local freelancer and small-agency rates. No live market data was checked.

## Scope priced

Software only. The client supplies the PC, camera and printer. Payment is GCash/Maya QR (no cash or coin acceptor). Output is a printed copy and a soft copy (QR download, email, cloud gallery).

## Market range (software only)

| | Freelancer / solo dev | Small team / agency |
|---|---|---|
| Full feature set | ₱108k – 230k | ₱215k – 440k |

Suggested payment terms: 30% downpayment, 40% at working demo, 30% at turnover.

## With AI-assisted development

Same full scope: about 248 h, ₱111,600, 6–8 weeks. Testing, camera, printer and real payment work barely shrink.

| Option | Price | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Full scope, no AI | ₱180,000 | ~3 months |
| Full scope, AI-assisted | ₱110k – 120k | 6–8 weeks |
| Lean MVP, AI-assisted | ₱60k – 80k | 3–4 weeks |

Recommended quote when selling it: ₱130k–150k for full scope, ₱60k–80k for the lean MVP.

The detailed plans came out larger than the rough estimate:

| | Lean MVP | Full scope |
|---|---|---|
| Tasks | 14 | 25 |
| Hours | ~172 | ~299 |
| Cost at ₱450/h | ~₱77,400 | ~₱134,550 |
| Time | 4–5 weeks | 8–9 weeks |

Warranty: 30 days of free bug fixes after turnover.

## Recurring costs (client pays)

- Hosting, domain and cloud storage: about ₱500–2,000 a month.
- PayMongo transaction fees. The client's business needs DTI or SEC registration.
- Maintenance: 10–20% of the build price per year.

## Where it runs

- Kiosk app: Windows 10/11 64-bit only.
- Admin panel and download page: any browser.
- Not supported: phones, tablets, Mac and Linux booths.
